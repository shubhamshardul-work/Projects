## 🔬 Research Frontiers

### **[VISTA: A Visual Harness for Reasoning in an Interactive World](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02200v1)**
The introduction of VISTA showcases a significant leap in multimodal models' ability to reason and solve tasks in complex, interactive environments by providing an appropriate visual harness. This research highlights the potential for AI to move beyond static analysis into dynamic, real-world problem-solving, paving the way for more capable and adaptable intelligent agents.

### **[TACO: Ternary Absolute-max Column-wise One-sparse Optimizer for LLM Fine-Tuning](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02199v1)**
TACO presents an innovative solution to the substantial memory overhead of fine-tuning large language models, a critical bottleneck for researchers and practitioners. By compressing optimizer states, this work promises to unlock the ability to fine-tune even larger LLMs on existing hardware, accelerating research and development in model adaptation.

### **[One Basis to Animate Them All: Gaussian Blendshape Distillation for Real-Time Avatars](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02207v1)**
This research addresses the challenge of real-time animation for 3D Gaussian avatars, a crucial step towards highly realistic and interactive virtual characters. By distilling animation into a single basis, it offers a path to significantly faster rendering and animation, which has profound implications for virtual reality, gaming, and digital content creation.

### **[KaliBench: A Fine-Grained Benchmark for Cybersecurity Tool Use on Kali Linux with Runtime-Free Verifiable Rewards](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02206v1)**
KaliBench introduces a much-needed fine-grained benchmark for evaluating LLMs in cybersecurity workflows, moving beyond knowledge-based assessments to practical tool invocation. This work is vital for developing and validating AI agents capable of assisting or automating complex cybersecurity tasks, ensuring their reliability and effectiveness in critical applications.

### **[With most information hidden, the game Stratego had stumped AI until now](https://arstechnica.com/science/2026/10/02/ai-finally-beat-the-best-stratego-player-in-history-and-did-it-on-a-budget/)**
The breakthrough in AI mastering Stratego, a game characterized by imperfect information, marks a significant milestone in AI's ability to handle complex strategic reasoning under uncertainty. This achievement demonstrates AI's growing capacity to excel in scenarios where human intuition and deception play a crucial role, opening new avenues for AI application in strategic planning and decision-making.
