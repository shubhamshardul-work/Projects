## 🔬 Research Frontiers

### **[VISTA: A Visual Harness for Reasoning in an Interactive World](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02200v1)**
VISTA introduces a novel framework demonstrating how multimodal models can unlock powerful reasoning abilities across diverse interactive environments. This research pushes the boundaries of AI's capacity to understand and operate within complex visual and interactive contexts, paving the way for more intelligent and adaptable embodied AI systems.

### **[Reconstruct, Practice, Go Real: Guided Self-Improvement for Embodied Agents](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02204v1)**
This paper presents a compelling approach to building reliable robot capabilities through guided self-improvement, significantly reducing the human effort typically required for skill development and reward design. The "Reconstruct, Practice, Go Real" paradigm offers a promising path towards more autonomous and adaptable embodied agents capable of mastering complex tasks in the real world.

### **[KaliBench: A Fine-Grained Benchmark for Cybersecurity Tool Use on Kali Linux with Runtime-Free Verifiable Rewards](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02206v1)**
KaliBench addresses a critical gap in evaluating LLMs for cybersecurity workflows by providing a fine-grained benchmark for tool invocation on Kali Linux. This research is vital for developing and assessing AI agents that can effectively translate human intent into practical cybersecurity actions, enhancing automation and efficiency in threat detection and response.

### **[One Basis to Animate Them All: Gaussian Blendshape Distillation for Real-Time Avatars](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02207v1)**
This research tackles the bottleneck of real-time animation for 3D Gaussian avatars by introducing Gaussian Blendshape Distillation. By enabling fast rendering and animation of complex avatars, this work has significant implications for virtual reality, gaming, and digital communication, promising more immersive and responsive interactive experiences.

### **[Embedding Prediction Helps Image Generation](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02203v1)**
Exploring whether predicted embeddings can serve as conditions in diffusion transformers, this research offers a novel perspective on optimizing image generation processes. By potentially refining how conditions are applied at each denoising step, it could lead to more efficient and higher-quality generative AI models, impacting creative industries and synthetic data generation.
