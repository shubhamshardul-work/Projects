## 🔬 Research Frontiers

### **[Medical Image Segmentation: Foundation Model Adaptation versus Task-Specific Training](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14519v1)**
This paper delves into a critical question for medical AI: whether adapting large foundation models or training task-specific models is superior for medical image segmentation. The findings will have significant implications for resource allocation and methodological choices in developing AI for healthcare, guiding researchers on the most effective pathways to achieve robust and accurate diagnostic tools.

### **[Toward Foundation Detection Models via Example-Based Grounding in EM-DETR](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14518v1)**
This research explores the development of "Foundation Detection Models" for computer-aided diagnostics, aiming to reduce reliance on vast, perfectly annotated datasets. By leveraging example-based grounding, EM-DETR could significantly accelerate the deployment of AI in medical imaging, making advanced diagnostic tools more accessible and adaptable to real-world clinical scenarios with limited data.

### **[AutoScaffold: AI-Guided Scaffolding for Reinforcement Learning of Language Models](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14515v1)**
AutoScaffold proposes an innovative approach to reinforcement learning for language models by using AI-guided scaffolding, moving beyond simple outcome rewards to leverage richer training experiences. This method could lead to more efficient and effective training of complex language models, enabling them to learn nuanced behaviors and reasoning capabilities more rapidly and robustly.

### **[Contrastive Discovery: Open-Ended Scientific Discovery over Competing Explanations](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14514v1)**
This paper introduces "Contrastive Discovery," a novel framework for LLM agents to conduct open-ended scientific discovery by evaluating hypotheses against competing explanations, rather than just their complements. This approach promises to enhance the rigor and depth of AI-driven scientific exploration, potentially leading to more robust theories and accelerating the pace of discovery across various scientific domains.

### **[Pyrite: Building and Benchmarking Protein-Ligand Pose Ranking Agents](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14511v1)**
Pyrite addresses a crucial challenge in drug discovery: reliably ranking protein-ligand binding poses generated by cofolding models. By building and benchmarking specialized agents for this task, this research aims to improve the accuracy and efficiency of identifying promising drug candidates, potentially accelerating the development of new therapeutics and reducing the costs associated with early-stage drug design.
