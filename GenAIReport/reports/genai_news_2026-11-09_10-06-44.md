### **[CPU-ARC: Agentic Reasoning Caching for on-CPU Reasoning Reuse](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09891v1)**
This research introduces CPU-ARC, a novel approach to improve the efficiency of large language models by caching and reusing reasoning steps, rather than solving every problem from scratch. This method promises to significantly reduce inference costs and computational load, making agentic workflows more practical on less powerful, CPU-based hardware.

### **[CodeQuant2: Enhanced Outlier Mitigation and Adaptive Clustering for Low-Precision LRMs](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09868v1)**
CodeQuant2 addresses the critical challenge of quantizing Large Reasoning Models (LRMs) to lower precision without sacrificing performance, which is essential for reducing inference costs. By enhancing outlier mitigation and employing adaptive clustering, this work aims to make powerful LRMs more deployable and cost-effective for real-world applications.

## 🔬 Research Frontiers

### **[On Agentic Manipulation Policies](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09892v1)**
This paper explores the efficacy of generalist AI models in controlling robotic manipulators, demonstrating that they can achieve more reliable performance than traditional specialist policies. This research suggests a promising shift towards more adaptable and broadly capable robotic systems, potentially simplifying deployment in complex, unstructured environments.

### **[FlowMoP: Stochastic Multi-Person Motion Prediction](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09882v1)**
FlowMoP tackles the inherently challenging problem of predicting the future motion of multiple interacting people, acknowledging the stochastic nature of human behavior. By modeling diverse plausible continuations, this work is crucial for advancing applications in autonomous systems, human-robot interaction, and comprehensive social scene understanding.

### **[Grasp-TAGS: Task-Aware Grasp Selection via Critic Distillation for Prehensile Loco-Manipulation](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09864v1)**
Grasp-TAGS introduces an innovative method for mobile manipulators to select grasps that are not only stable but also optimized for the entire long-horizon task, rather than just the immediate object interaction. This advancement is key for improving the capabilities of robots in complex applications like service robotics and large-object rearrangement.

### **[Graded convolutional networks: scale invariance without the scale and robustness to unknown blur](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09878v1)**
This research presents graded convolutional networks, a novel architecture that inherently achieves scale invariance and robustness to unknown blur without requiring explicit data augmentation or complex multi-scale processing. This fundamental improvement could lead to more robust and efficient computer vision models, particularly in real-world scenarios with varying image conditions.

### **[On the Reliability of Majority-Vote Pseudo-Labels in Test-Time Reinforcement Learning](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09872v1)**
This paper investigates the reliability of self-generated pseudo-labels, particularly those derived from majority voting, in Test-Time Reinforcement Learning (TTRL) for adapting pretrained language models. Understanding and improving the trustworthiness of these labels is critical for the practical and robust application of TTRL in diverse, unlabeled target domains.
