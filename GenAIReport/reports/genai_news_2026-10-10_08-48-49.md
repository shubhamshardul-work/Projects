This paper tackles the fundamental challenge of exploration in large-scale reinforcement learning for general-purpose robot control, proposing methods for a "balanced data diet." Researchers in robotics and RL will find this vital for developing more efficient and robust training methodologies, accelerating the development of versatile robots capable of performing a wide range of complex tasks in real-world environments.

### **[On the estimation and validity of AI time horizons—a statistical look at the METR plot](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12466v1)**
This statistical examination of the METR plot provides a rigorous framework for estimating and validating AI time horizons, measuring human completion time of software tasks that AI solves with 50% probability. This work is crucial for researchers and policymakers seeking to accurately quantify AI capabilities and predict future advancements in an interpretable and statistically sound manner.

### **[CSF: Contextual Safety Filtering for Motion Generators](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12467v1)**
This research introduces Contextual Safety Filtering (CSF) for text-conditioned motion generators, addressing the critical gap where existing safeguards fail to account for scene-dependent safety. For researchers in generative AI and human-robot interaction, CSF offers a novel approach to ensure that AI-generated motions are not only realistic but also contextually safe, preventing potentially hazardous interactions in dynamic environments.
