## 🔬 Research Frontiers

### **[How to Train a Critic Stably and Efficiently](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23566v1)**
This paper tackles a fundamental challenge in reinforcement learning for large language models: the stable and efficient training of a critic. By proposing methods to improve critic reliability, this research could pave the way for more sophisticated and less sample-intensive RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) techniques, ultimately leading to more aligned and capable AI agents.

### **[ReWorld: An Interactive World Model with Long-Horizon Memory](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23565v1)**
ReWorld introduces an interactive world model designed to address the tension between short-horizon control and long-horizon memory in AI agents. This work is crucial for developing AI systems that can maintain context, remember past interactions, and operate effectively over extended periods in dynamic environments, pushing the boundaries for embodied AI and complex simulation.

### **[SWE Refactor Bench: Can Coding Agents Complete a Long-Horizon, Whole-Repository Stack Migration?](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23564v1)**
This research explores the ambitious goal of using coding agents to automate complex, long-horizon software engineering tasks like whole-repository stack migrations. The SWE Refactor Bench provides a benchmark for evaluating AI's capability to tackle technical debt, offering insights into the future potential for AI to significantly reduce manual effort in large-scale software maintenance and evolution.

### **[Provably adaptive sampling with uniform and remasking discrete diffusion models](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23554v1)**
This paper advances the field of discrete diffusion models by introducing provably adaptive sampling techniques. By improving sampling efficiency without sacrificing generation quality, this research contributes to making diffusion models more practical and scalable for generating discrete data, with implications for areas like text, code, and structured data synthesis.

### **[Prime Agent: A Self-Improving RLM Harness](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23552v1)**
Prime Agent presents an open-source harness for developing long-horizon, self-improving language model agents that can leverage external information and computation. This work is pivotal for moving beyond static language models towards truly agentic AI systems capable of complex problem-solving, continuous learning, and interaction with the real world.
