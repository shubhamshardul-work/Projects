This ArXiv paper investigates how subtle gradient edits can significantly improve the reasoning capabilities of smaller language models, preventing "thinking loops" and boosting accuracy. The findings offer crucial insights into fine-tuning LLM behavior and optimizing performance, even for resource-constrained models, suggesting that targeted interventions can have disproportionately large impacts on model efficacy.
