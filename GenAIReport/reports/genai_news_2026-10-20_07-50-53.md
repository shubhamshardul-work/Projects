## 🔬 Research Frontiers

### **[Where is Physical RSI capability from? An Observational Scaling Law of LLM-as-policy](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26848v1)**
This paper delves into the mechanisms and scaling behavior of physical Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI) for LLMs acting as policies, introducing PhysRSI-eval to quantify this capability. For researchers, understanding the origins and limits of physical RSI is crucial for developing truly autonomous and adaptive AI systems that can learn and improve in real-world environments, pushing the boundaries of robotics and embodied AI.

### **[OpenVLHarness: Real World Visual Agentic Tool-Use with Multimodal Memory](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26860v1)**
OpenVLHarness explores real-world visual agentic tool-use combined with multimodal memory, addressing the challenge of AI agents requiring external evidence beyond internal reasoning. This research is vital for advancing AI agents that can effectively interact with complex visual environments, remember past interactions, and utilize tools to achieve goals, moving closer to human-like problem-solving capabilities.

### **[Can It Actually Work? Synthesizing Functional CAD Mechanisms](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26861v1)**
This study investigates the synthesis of functional CAD mechanisms, tackling the critical gap where AI-generated designs often lack physical functionality. For deep-tech thinkers, this work is paramount for realizing the promise of AI in engineering and manufacturing, enabling AI agents to not just design visually plausible objects but to create physically viable and operational mechanisms.

### **[Text-to-Image Generative Modeling Without Language Pretraining](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26863v1)**
Challenging the conventional wisdom, this paper explores text-to-image generative modeling *without* relying on internet-scale language pretraining. This research could lead to more efficient, specialized, or novel approaches to image generation, potentially reducing computational overhead or enabling new forms of multimodal understanding that are not tethered to existing language models.

### **[AnyGoal: On World Model Generalization Beyond Expert Rollouts](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26844v1)**
AnyGoal focuses on improving the generalization of world models beyond expert rollouts, a fundamental challenge in reinforcement learning. For researchers, enhancing world model generalization is key to developing more robust and versatile AI agents that can plan and simulate actions effectively in novel or complex environments, accelerating progress towards general artificial intelligence.
