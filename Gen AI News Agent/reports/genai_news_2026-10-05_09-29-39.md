## 🔬 Research Frontiers

### **[4DCodeBench: Benchmarking Agents on Inverse Graphics of Dynamic Scenes](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03715v1)**
Researchers introduce 4DCodeBench, a novel benchmark designed to evaluate AI agents' ability to reconstruct dynamic 3D scenes from video by generating executable graphics programs. This work pushes the boundaries of inverse graphics, offering a new tool for assessing and advancing AI's understanding and representation of complex, changing environments, crucial for robotics and advanced simulation.

### **[EyeRobot 2.0: Active Gaze for Precise Manipulation without Wrist Cameras](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03710v1)**
Inspired by human vision, EyeRobot 2.0 presents a framework for precise bimanual robotic manipulation using only a single stereo camera and active gaze. This innovation demonstrates a path towards more efficient and less sensor-heavy robotic systems, potentially simplifying hardware requirements and enhancing dexterity for real-world applications by mimicking biological visual strategies.

### **[RNADyn: A Benchmark for Generating and Understanding RNA Dynamics](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03712v1)**
The introduction of RNADyn addresses a significant gap in bioinformatics by providing a benchmark for generating and understanding RNA dynamics, which are crucial for their function but often overlooked by static structures. This resource will accelerate research into RNA conformational changes, opening new avenues for drug discovery and synthetic biology by enabling more accurate modeling of molecular processes.

### **[LESSER: Post-Training Data Selection with Output-Layer Gradients](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03702v1)**
LESSER proposes a novel method for post-training data selection for large language models (LLMs) by leveraging output-layer gradients to rank training data effectiveness. This research offers a powerful technique for optimizing LLM performance and efficiency, allowing practitioners to intelligently curate data for fine-tuning and deployment, thereby improving model quality with less data.

### **[Less Decoder is More Encoder: Geometric Representation Learning from Novel View Synthesis](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03717v1)**
This paper challenges conventional wisdom in neural rendering by demonstrating that a "less decoder is more encoder" approach can significantly improve geometric representation learning from novel view synthesis. The findings suggest a more efficient and robust pathway to learning transferable multi-view geometric representations, with profound implications for fields like augmented reality, virtual reality, and autonomous navigation.
