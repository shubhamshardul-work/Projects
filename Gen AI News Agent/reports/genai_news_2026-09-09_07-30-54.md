### **[On the Navier–Stokes Millennium Prize Problem](https://openai.com/index/navier-stokes-solution)**

### **[How GPT-5.6 Sol helps run quantum computing experiments](https://openai.com/index/codex-quantum-computing-experiments)**
An MIT researcher is leveraging GPT-5.6 Sol with Codex to autonomously conduct quantum computing experiments, analyze results, and calibrate qubits. This showcases AI's transformative potential in accelerating scientific research, automating complex experimental setups, and bridging the gap between classical AI and the nascent field of quantum computing.

### **[TANGO: Humanoid Navigation in Cluttered Environments with a Whole-Body Vision-Language-Action Model](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09158v1)**
This research introduces TANGO, a novel approach for humanoid robots to navigate cluttered indoor environments using a whole-body vision-language-action model. Unlike traditional 2D path planning, TANGO's multi-modal integration pushes the boundaries of embodied AI, offering significant implications for autonomous robotics in complex, real-world settings.

### **[Procedural Graphs: Self-Evolving Execution Structures for LLM Agents](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09153v1)**
This paper explores Procedural Graphs as self-evolving execution structures for LLM agents, enhancing their ability to plan over long horizons and interact with external tools. This work is crucial for developing more robust, adaptive, and autonomous AI agents capable of tackling complex, multi-step tasks without constant human oversight.

### **[Large language models develop novel social biases through adaptive exploration](https://openreview.net/challenge?redirect=%2Fforum%3Fid%3Dpc7fqaOcAH)**
Research indicates that large language models can develop novel social biases through adaptive exploration, highlighting emergent and often unpredictable behaviors. This finding underscores the critical need for ongoing research into AI ethics, bias detection, and control mechanisms to ensure fairness and safety as LLMs become more integrated into societal functions.
