## 🔬 Research Frontiers

### **[A step toward defining the Standard Model on a lattice](https://openai.com/index/step-toward-defining-the-standard-model)**
OpenAI's research explores a candidate nonperturbative lattice definition of the Standard Model, showcasing how advanced AI like GPT-6 Astra can accelerate complex theoretical physics proofs. This work represents a significant leap in using AI for fundamental scientific discovery, potentially unlocking new avenues for understanding the universe's most basic principles. It highlights AI's growing role as a co-pilot in high-level scientific reasoning.

### **[ArchSpace: Recording, Reviewing, and Referencing LLM Architecture Research](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17332v2)**
ArchSpace introduces a crucial framework for systematically recording, reviewing, and referencing LLM architecture research, addressing the rapid evolution and complexity of model design. This initiative is vital for the academic and industrial communities to track, understand, and build upon foundational architectural innovations, ultimately shaping the scaling behavior, training efficiency, and inference costs of future LLMs. It's a meta-research effort essential for organizing the LLM knowledge base.

### **[WeightBench: Evaluating Language Models as Weight-Space Programmers](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18553v1)**
WeightBench proposes a novel paradigm where LLMs are evaluated for their ability to directly operate on the parameters (weights) of other neural networks, effectively acting as "weight-space programmers." This groundbreaking research suggests a future where AI can not only understand but also modify and optimize other AI systems, opening doors to self-improving models and advanced meta-learning capabilities. It challenges our understanding of AI's agency and control.

### **[FETTUCCINE: Fast and efficient brain-to-text decoding on mobile devices](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18561v1)**
The FETTUCCINE project presents a significant advancement in brain-to-text decoding, enabling fast and efficient neural communication on mobile devices. This research has profound implications for individuals with neurological illnesses or injuries, offering new avenues for communication and accessibility. It pushes the boundaries of neuroprostheses, bringing sophisticated brain-computer interfaces closer to practical, portable applications.

### **[Training Reasoning Models in Simulation to Natively Use Real Robots](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18562v1)**
This research addresses a critical challenge in robotics: bridging the gap between simulated training and real-world robot deployment for general-purpose control. By training reasoning models in simulation to natively use real robots, the work aims to overcome limitations of proprietary models like GPT-6 Astra, making advanced robot-use agents more accessible and robust. It's a key step towards more adaptable and autonomous robotic systems.
