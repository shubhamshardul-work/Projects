## 🔬 Research Frontiers

### **[Discovering state-of-the-art quantum circuits with large language models](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22367v1)**
This research explores a fascinating intersection of AI and quantum computing, leveraging large language models to discover efficient quantum circuits. By automating the design of complex quantum algorithms, this approach could significantly accelerate the development of useful quantum advantage. It represents a novel pathway for AI to contribute to fundamental scientific discovery in emerging fields.

### **[LIBERO-PeRM: Benchmarking Personalized Robotic Manipulation](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22372v1)**
LIBERO-PeRM addresses the crucial challenge of personalization in general-purpose robotics, a key step towards practical household robots. This benchmark focuses on enabling robots to adapt to individual user preferences and diverse home environments, moving beyond generalized manipulation tasks. The research is vital for developing robots that can truly integrate into human lives and perform tasks tailored to specific needs.

### **[Scaling Multilingual Mixture-of-Experts: How Language Coverage Changes Capacity and Compute Allocation](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20247v2)**
This paper investigates the efficient scaling of multilingual models using Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures, specifically how language coverage impacts model capacity and computational allocation. The findings are critical for designing more inclusive and performant global AI systems that can handle a vast array of languages without compromising performance on existing ones. It offers insights into optimizing resource allocation for diverse linguistic datasets.

### **[T2-z: Test-Time Optimization of Environment Latents for Robotic World Models](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22364v1)**
T2-z introduces a method for robots to optimize unobservable environmental factors at test time, enhancing the predictive power of robotic world models. This research is crucial for enabling robots to operate robustly in complex, real-world scenarios where complete information is rarely available, allowing them to infer hidden physics or properties. It pushes the boundaries of robotic perception and decision-making in uncertain environments.

### **[FDE-Gym: The Last Mile of AI Delivery](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22350v1)**
FDE-Gym highlights the critical gap between academic AI benchmarks and the realities of real-world customer delivery and forward-deployed engineering. This research emphasizes the need for evaluating AI agents in dynamic, ill-specified scenarios that reflect actual customer needs and evolving requirements. It pushes the research community to focus on the practical challenges of deploying and maintaining AI in production environments.
