This work deepens our understanding of optimal decision-making under uncertainty, with potential applications in reinforcement learning and experimental design.

This theoretical computer science paper explores the fundamental limits and properties of language generation, characterizing the task for arbitrary language families. It contributes to a deeper understanding of what AI can achieve in language tasks and the inherent complexities involved.
