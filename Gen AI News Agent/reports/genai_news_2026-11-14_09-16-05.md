## 🔬 Research Frontiers

### **[Unbundling Multimodal Vision Encoding](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17334v1)**
This paper delves into a fundamental architectural question for multimodal AI: how best to integrate visual information. The debate between using pre-trained vision encoders versus training them from scratch with language models has significant implications for model efficiency, data requirements, and the ultimate performance of future multimodal systems, pointing to ongoing efforts to optimize how AI "sees" and understands the world.

### **[ArchSpace: Recording, Reviewing, and Referencing LLM Architecture Research](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17332v1)**
ArchSpace highlights the critical importance of systematic research into LLM architectures, which directly impacts scaling behavior, training efficiency, and inference costs. This initiative aims to provide a framework for understanding and comparing different architectural innovations, crucial for guiding future development and making informed design choices in the rapidly evolving LLM landscape.

### **[AgentSSL: Can MLE Agents Leverage Unlabeled Data?](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17323v1)**
This research explores the potential for Machine Learning Engineering (MLE) agents to utilize unlabeled data, addressing a major bottleneck in applying ML systems to new domains. If successful, this could significantly reduce the need for costly labeled datasets and manual pipeline engineering, accelerating the deployment of AI in diverse, data-scarce environments.

### **[Learning When Diffusion Language Models Should Regenerate](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17320v1)**
This paper tackles the challenge of improving diffusion language models by teaching them *when* and *what* to revise during text generation. Understanding how to effectively leverage remasking for correction could lead to more accurate, coherent, and robust text generation, pushing the boundaries of what these models can achieve in creative and analytical tasks.

### **[Classical Energies Learn to Reason 1,000x Faster than Looped Models](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17309v1)**
This intriguing research proposes a novel approach to reasoning that significantly outperforms traditional looped models in speed. By drawing inspiration from classical energy principles, it suggests a fundamentally different paradigm for AI reasoning, potentially unlocking new avenues for developing more efficient and stable AI systems capable of complex problem-solving.
