## 🔬 Research Frontiers

### **[Latent Lattice Language Models](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21608v1)**
This paper proposes a novel approach to language model architecture, moving beyond traditional deep or looped layers by exploring richer residual connections and latent reasoning steps. This aims to improve information flow and computational efficiency, potentially leading to more powerful and nuanced language understanding.

### **[ViRA: Toward Ultra-High-Resolution Visual Agents via Cross-Input Relative Policy Optimization](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21605v1)**
ViRA tackles the challenge of visual agents answering questions about ultra-high-resolution images by intelligently zooming into relevant regions. This research focuses on improving evidence-based reasoning rather than relying on common sense, which is vital for applications requiring precise visual analysis and detailed contextual understanding.

### **[How VLMs Plan What's Next](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21600v1)**
This study delves into the internal mechanisms of Vision-Language Models (VLMs), specifically characterizing how they plan the order of object descriptions without repetition. Understanding this planning process can lead to more coherent, natural, and efficient VLM outputs, enhancing their utility in complex descriptive tasks.

### **[Hyperlyve: Parallelized Enumeration and Exact Lyapunov Stability Certification for Deep ReLU Networks](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21599v1)**
Hyperlyve introduces a framework for proving the asymptotic stability of nonlinear dynamical systems, including those powered by deep ReLU networks. This is a significant step towards ensuring the reliability and safety of AI systems in critical applications where stability guarantees are paramount.

### **[Mitigating Catastrophic Forgetting with Physics-Indexed Specialist Memory: the Evolutionary Neuroplastic Agent](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21598v1)**
This research addresses catastrophic forgetting in continual reinforcement learning by proposing an Evolutionary Neuroplastic Agent with physics-indexed specialist memory. This approach aims to enable AI systems to adapt to new conditions while retaining previously learned knowledge, crucial for robust, lifelong learning agents.
