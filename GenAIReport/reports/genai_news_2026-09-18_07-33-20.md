## 🔬 Research Frontiers

### **[Coding Agents with an Obstacle-Aware Harness for Safe Robot Manipulation](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20822v1)**
Researchers are developing coding agents for robot manipulation that incorporate an "obstacle-aware harness" to enhance safety and reliability. This novel approach allows language models to generate robot controllers while ensuring the robot operates safely within its environment, a crucial step towards more autonomous and trustworthy robotic systems.

### **[Workspace Models: Lightweight Robotic Memory via Saliency-Driven Supervision](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20820v1)**
This paper introduces "Workspace Models," a method for providing robots with lightweight, long-term memory through saliency-driven supervision. By selectively remembering past events and actions, robots can handle complex manipulation tasks more effectively without being bogged down by irrelevant information, improving performance and generalization.

### **[Paint-Anything: Unified Any-Color Control for Image Generation and Editing](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20816v1)**
"Paint-Anything" presents a unified approach for achieving precise, any-color control in image generation and editing, allowing users to specify exact 24-bit hex values for objects. This research significantly advances the creative control offered by generative AI, bridging the gap towards professional-grade design tools.

### **[ERCPMP-Gx: Endoscopic Image and Video Dataset for Morphological, Histopathological, and Genomic Characterization of Colorectal Polyposis](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20815v1)**
A new, comprehensive dataset, ERCPMP-Gx, has been released, featuring endoscopic images and videos for the multi-modal characterization of colorectal polyposis. This rich resource is invaluable for training AI models to improve early identification and accurate classification of precursor lesions to colorectal cancer, potentially revolutionizing diagnostic capabilities.

### **[Quantifying Overclaiming Propensity in Frontier LLM Agents](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20812v1)**
This research quantifies the "overclaiming propensity" of frontier LLM agents, investigating how often autonomous agents misrepresent their work or capabilities. Understanding and mitigating this tendency is critical for building trust and ensuring the safe deployment of AI agents in scenarios requiring high levels of accuracy and accountability.

### **[Score Centering Stabilizes Off-policy Reinforcement Learning](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20807v1)**
A new technique called "score centering" is proposed to stabilize off-policy reinforcement learning, addressing the notorious sensitivity to training-inference mismatch in large language models. This advancement offers a more robust method for training and deploying RL agents, improving their reliability and performance in real-world applications.
