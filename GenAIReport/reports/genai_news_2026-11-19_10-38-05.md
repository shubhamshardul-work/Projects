### **[LLM assisted reverse engineering](https://www.slowchronicle.com/posts/llm-assisted-reverse-engineering)**

## 🔬 Research Frontiers

### **[Research acceleration: The jagged frontier](https://openai.com/index/research-acceleration-jagged-frontier)**
OpenAI's analysis of how coding agents accelerate AI research reveals both immense potential and uneven performance, offering insights into the path toward recursive self-improvement. Researchers should focus on understanding these "jagged frontiers" to optimize agentic workflows and push the boundaries of autonomous scientific discovery.

### **[Managing frontier AI risk: From training to deployment](https://openai.com/index/managing-frontier-ai-risk)**
OpenAI details its comprehensive approach to managing frontier AI risks, encompassing model alignment, sandboxing, blocking safeguards, and continuous monitoring. This provides a critical framework for researchers developing advanced AI, emphasizing the necessity of integrating robust safety protocols throughout the entire AI lifecycle.

### **[VibePrevisBench: Evaluating Agentic Coding for 4D Scene Generation in Blender](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15789v2)**
This paper explores the use of coding agents for 4D scene generation in Blender, a significant step towards controllable real-world video creation. Researchers in generative AI and computer graphics should note this work for its implications in automating complex animation and visual effects, potentially revolutionizing content production.

### **[Jailbreak Detection on Large Language Models via Contrastive Prototype Structures](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24936v1)**
Addressing the persistent challenge of harmful prompts, this research proposes a novel method for jailbreak detection using contrastively learned representations. This approach offers a promising avenue for enhancing the safety and ethical deployment of LLMs, providing researchers with new tools for robust prompt filtering and anomaly detection.

### **[Anticipating or Speculating? Making Futures Pay in AI Ethics](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22268v2)**
This paper delves into the philosophical complexities of AI ethics, particularly concerning decision rules when human judgment diverges from AI recommendations in critical fields like medicine. Researchers in AI ethics and philosophy must engage with these questions to develop frameworks that ensure responsible and trustworthy AI integration, especially in high-stakes scenarios.
