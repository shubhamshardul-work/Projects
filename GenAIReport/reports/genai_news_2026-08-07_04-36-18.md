### **[Humans missed 1 in 3 threats approving AI agent commands across 40k game runs](https://scalex.dev/blog/ai-agent-permissions-stats/)**
This critical finding highlights a significant vulnerability in human-supervised AI agent systems: human operators are prone to missing substantial threats when approving agent commands. ## 🔬 Research Frontiers

### **[Learning When to Trust via Selective Context Preference Optimization](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06377v1)**
This research tackles the crucial problem of language models' susceptibility to misleading external signals, proposing a method to train models to selectively trust information. For researchers, this offers a promising avenue for developing more robust and reliable AI systems that can discern credible context, moving closer to truly trustworthy AI.

### **[The Bitter Lesson of Tool Calling](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06370v1)**
This paper explores the evolution of LLM tool use, moving beyond rigid JSON calls to more flexible, programmatic scripting. Researchers should watch this space for new paradigms in agentic AI, as it suggests a future where LLMs can dynamically generate and execute complex scripts, vastly expanding their capabilities and autonomy.

### **[AV-AIVAT: 74x Cheaper Agent Evaluation with Certified Anytime-Valid Stopping in Imperfect-Information Games](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06362v1)**
This work introduces a significantly more efficient method for evaluating AI agents in complex game environments, drastically reducing the cost and time required. For academics and practitioners, AV-AIVAT provides a powerful tool to accelerate agent development and comparison, enabling faster iteration and more rigorous validation of AI agent performance.

### **[Tracing the Heart: An Evidence-Linked Pipeline for Heart-Failure Feature Engineering](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06366v1)**
Addressing a major bottleneck in clinical AI, this research presents an evidence-linked pipeline for feature engineering in heart failure research using electronic health records. This offers a blueprint for researchers to streamline data preparation in complex medical domains, potentially accelerating the development of life-saving AI applications in healthcare.

### **[The Low Frequency Trap: Video Language Models Fail at Simple Event Bookkeeping](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06361v1)**
This paper identifies a fundamental limitation in current Video Language Models (VLMs), revealing their struggle with basic event counting and bookkeeping in videos. Researchers should note this "low frequency trap" as a critical area for improvement, guiding the development of more sophisticated VLM architectures capable of nuanced temporal understanding.
